        What the sim does and doesn't model

        Modeled from the instruction sheet and the photos: sell by weight, sell by PLU number, manual unit price, TARE, ZERO, SAVE (hold the price for repeat labels), AUTO, quantity with the times key, the price-change and label procedures, and the test sticker.

        Guesses: the menu words between PLU and NAME, and between the PLU record's NAME and PRICE and SAVE, show as dashes because the sheet doesn't say what they are. A name saved through the label procedure is used on labels printed without a PLU. FOR, 1/4, and 1/2 are not modeled; find out what they do at the shop and I'll add them.

        Practice times each answer silently. A key you get right fast comes back in a while; slow or wrong keys come back within a few prompts. Progress, points, badges, and Rush high scores are saved on this phone (per player name). In Procedures, the finish line names your slowest step, and a clean run in Test mode earns the Procedure pro badge.

        Prices come from the Notion catalogue and refresh every time the site rebuilds (this build: __BUILT__, __PRICED__ keys priced). This page is public, so they stay locked until you enter the shop code once on your phone. Keys with no catalogue price show 0.00. Prices marked unconfirmed came off the price boards and need a check at the scale; prices marked cave sheet are primal prices, not case prices. Changing a price with the procedure sticks for the session only; the catalogue is the source of truth.

        Per-unit produce (pink keys)

        Shallot, avocado, lemon, lime, garlic, onion, apple, lettuce, corn, russet potato, asparagus ring by the piece: type the count, press the times key, then the product key.

        Key position is the PLU number: row 1 is 1 to 9, each row below adds 9.
